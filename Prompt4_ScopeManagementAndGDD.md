GDDs are critical for game dev studios when it comes to tracking their progress and updating their scope, two things which should be happening constantly. Very often, studios will end up becoming victims of their own ambition, something called Scope Creep. To combat this, it's important that when a mechanic seems like it's too much for the desired scope, the teams discusses how important it truly is, and if necessary, cross it out on the GDD. It's also extremely important to include your reasoning for cutting things out when you do this, so that you can look back at the GDD and remember your thought process.

The GDD should never just stay static, it should always reflect the current state of the project it exists to support. Include cut content, finished content, future plans, where every dev is assigned, and so on. Ideally, I would argue it should be updated every single week at least.
